## Findings

| Severity | Finding |
|---|---|
| Critical | **Homepage title is "Startseite"** — the single most valuable SEO element on the site says nothing. Google shows "Startseite" in SERPs. Should be e.g. "Restaurant zum goldenen Rössli – Gourmet-Restaurant & Boutique-Hotel in Wil SG". |
| Critical | **No H1 on any page.** All pages start at H2. |
| High | **One identical meta description on all 5 pages**, containing literal line breaks: "Restaurant\nGourmet\nschöne Ambiente\nBoutique Hotel" — keyword fragments, no sentence, no CTA, duplicated site-wide. |
| High | **No keyword targeting**: "Restaurant Wil", "italienisches Restaurant Wil SG", "Hotel Wil SG", "Gourmet Restaurant Ostschweiz" appear nowhere in titles/headings. |
| Medium | Thin content: hotel page 85 words, no room descriptions, no prices, no photos context, no booking info beyond phone. |
| Medium | No og:image → shares on WhatsApp/Facebook/Instagram show no preview image. og:type/locale missing. |
| Low | `meta keywords="Rössli"` — obsolete, harmless. |
| Low | Typo in footer NAP: "Toggenburgerstra**s**e 59" (should be Toggenburgerstrasse) — repeated on every page. |
